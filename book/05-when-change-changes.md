# 5. When Change Changes

Sugar Bear's cloud changed by 2 puffs, then by 1 puff.

The change itself changed:

\[
1-2=-1
\]

That is a **second difference**: the change of the change.

On a swing, your height changes. Near the top, the way your height changes also changes. That is why you slow down, stop for a blink, and swing back.

We can keep asking:

- What changed?
- Did the change change?
- Did *that* change change?

These are higher differences. We use them only when they help an observer notice the shape of change.

## Tiny lesson

First differences tell us about motion. Later differences tell us how that motion bends, speeds up, or settles down.
