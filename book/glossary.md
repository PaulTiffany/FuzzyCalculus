# Sugar Bear's Tiny Glossary

**Boundary**  
Where we choose to say something begins or ends.

**Degree**  
A number from 0 to 1 saying how strongly a description fits.

**Difference**  
The change between one state and the next.

**Drift**  
Change that gathers across steps.

**Fuzzy**  
Having measured degrees or soft distinctions—not random, careless, or confused.

**Identity**  
The rule by which an observer treats something as the same thing across change.

**Observer**  
Whatever measures, distinguishes, or interprets a change.

**Resolution**  
The smallest difference an observer can notice for the present purpose.

**Smooth**  
Changing without a jump large enough to matter under the chosen test and resolution.

**Transport**  
Moving meaning, evidence, or structure from one context to another.
