# 1. The Edge That Would Not Sit Still

Sugar Bear found a cloud resting in the berry field.

“I will draw your edge,” said Sugar Bear.

But the cloud puffed out here and tucked in there. Its edge would not sit still.

Yvonne brought a cookie. The cookie had a crisp edge. A crumb was either inside the cookie or outside it.

The cloud was different. Near its middle, it was very cloudy. Far away, it was not cloudy at all. Between those places, it was **a little cloudy**.

Sugar Bear stopped drawing one hard line.

Instead, Sugar Bear asked:

> How much does this spot belong to the cloud?

That is the first fuzzy question.

We can answer with numbers from 0 to 1:

- **0** means “not part of it.”
- **1** means “fully part of it.”
- **0.5** means “halfway.”

The cloud did not lose its edge. Its edge became a gentle region instead of one sharp line.

## Tiny lesson

A fuzzy edge is not a mistake. Sometimes it is a better description of what is really there.
