# 4. Footprints of Change

Every morning, Sugar Bear measured the cloud's puffiness.

| Morning | Puffiness |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 4 |
| 2 | 6 |
| 3 | 7 |

From Morning 1 to Morning 2, puffiness changed by:

\[
6-4=2
\]

From Morning 2 to Morning 3, it changed by:

\[
7-6=1
\]

Each subtraction is a **difference**. A difference is the footprint left by change.

An observer's difference tool can be called \(\delta_O\). It asks, “How much change can observer \(O\) see?”

If Little Bear notices only changes of 2 or more, the first change is visible and the second is not.

The cloud changed both times. But it did not change *for every observer* both times.

## Tiny lesson

Calculus begins by following change. Fuzzy calculus also remembers who is doing the following.

## Honey check

A plant grows 3 centimeters and then 1 centimeter. Which growth would a ruler marked only every 2 centimeters clearly show?
