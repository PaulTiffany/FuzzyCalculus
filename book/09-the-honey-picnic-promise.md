# 9. The Honey-Picnic Promise

Sugar Bear promised to bring honey to the picnic.

Then Sugar Bear changed, learned, traveled, forked into stories, and joined again.

The final bear remembered the promise—but remembering was not the same as keeping it.

Capability answers:

> Can the bear carry the honey?

Authority answers:

> May the bear change the promise?

Evidence answers:

> What shows what the bear actually did?

These are different questions. A bear becoming cleverer does not silently gain permission to rewrite every promise.

Yvonne checked the journey's receipts. Sugar Bear carried the honey. Everyone shared the picnic.

The cloud came too, though nobody could agree exactly where it ended.

That was all right.

## What Sugar Bear learned

1. Some edges are soft.
2. A degree can say how strongly a description fits.
3. Observers notice different amounts of change.
4. Differences are footprints of change.
5. Smoothness can depend on an observer's resolution.
6. Boundaries summarize journeys but can hide inside events.
7. Forking and merging do not automatically preserve identity.
8. Capability, authority, and evidence must not be mixed together.

The end is a boundary, not an eraser.
