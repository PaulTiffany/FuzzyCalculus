# 3. The Sugar Scale

Yvonne made tea. Sugar Bear wanted to describe how sweet it was.

“Sweet” or “not sweet” was too rough. So they made a sugar scale:

| Number | Bear words |
|---:|---|
| 0 | not sweet |
| 0.25 | a tiny bit sweet |
| 0.5 | halfway sweet |
| 0.75 | quite sweet |
| 1 | as sweet as our scale goes |

This number is called a **degree**. It says how strongly a description fits.

It does not mean Sugar Bear is confused. It means the answer has more room than yes or no.

If the tea is sweet to degree \(0.7\), then “not sweet” can be measured as:

\[
1-0.7=0.3
\]

Fuzzy descriptions can overlap. Tea may be warm to degree \(0.8\) and cozy to degree \(0.9\). The words measure different things.

## Tiny lesson

Fuzziness is measured softness, not muddle.

## Honey check

Choose a number from 0 to 1 for how sleepy you feel. Would another observer choose the same number?
