# Add a Little Honey

This book welcomes small, kind improvements.

Please keep every lesson:

- readable by an eighth-grade student or younger;
- honest about the mathematics;
- gentle toward readers who see things differently;
- free of unexplained symbols;
- centered on one new idea at a time.

If a technical term is necessary, introduce it first through a story or example. Then give it a name.

Original characters and artwork only, please.
